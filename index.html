Flights, Hotel Tariffs and APIs

How to book website flights and flight allocations, tell hotel tariffs and APIs apart, and handle packages, tours and last-minute bookings in UDMS.
Videos Quick Reference 1. Website Flights 2. Load in UDMS 3. Allocations 4. Tariffs 5. APIs 6. Packages 7. Tours 8. Last Minute Final Check
✈️

Not every flight or hotel is booked the same way. Some flights must be booked on the airline's own website, some come from our allocations, and hotels can come from a tariff (our loaded contract rates) or an API (live rates straight from the hotel). Knowing which is which keeps your quotes accurate and avoids clients arriving without tickets or rooms.

Part A

Flights

1

Website Flights: FastJet

Some regional airlines have their own website behind them. Most of the time FlySafair and LIFT pull through in UDMS as normal. FastJet is different: always book it on the FastJet agent portal, then load it in UDMS on Flight Carrier (Step 2). This mostly comes up with Victoria Falls.

⚠️
Do not book FastJet through Hahn AirFastJet can also show in API flights under Hahn Air. We have had bookings priced and ticketed on Hahn Air where the client got to the airport and there were no tickets, so the flights had to be rebooked on the FastJet website. Everything FastJet is booked on the website, for safety.
  • Rates come from the agent portal, using your own FastJet login.
  • The website is often much cheaper than the Hahn Air price, sometimes by R3,000 to R4,000 per person. Don't assume SAA is cheaper just because the Hahn Air price looks high; check FastJet.
  • Morning flights usually cost more. A slightly later flight can be cheaper and still gets clients there in time for afternoon plans like a sunset cruise.

On the FastJet results you'll see the flight number, date, departure and arrival times, then the net fare and airport taxes. Both are totals for all passengers, for that flight.

2

Load a Website Flight in UDMS

  1. Add up the totals from the website: both fares (out and back) together, and both airport tax amounts together.
  2. Create your quote as normal and add the passengers.
  3. Add the flight on Flight Carrier: return, airports, supplier FastJet, economy, both dates, departure and arrival times, and the flight numbers (for example FN 8502 out and FN 8501 back).
  4. Fare line: divide the total fare by the number of passengers and enter it per person. Leave the sell price blank so the 14% margin is added.

    Infants usually pay little more than one or two taxes, so work them out separately.

  5. Airport taxes as a separate line: under Extra, add an ad hoc for the taxes (same dates, supplier FastJet). Divide the total taxes per person and enter the same amount as buy and sell. We don't earn on airport taxes.
  6. Check the total against the website. Small rounding differences are normal; you can adjust them before you issue.
💡
Why fare and taxes are separateFlights that fall under Amadeus are paid through BSP, and Accounts pays the fare and the airport taxes separately. So for FastJet, FlySafair, LIFT and anything on Amadeus: fare first, airport taxes on their own line.
💱
The exception: flights in another currencyWhen a flight is booked on an airline's own website in another currency (for example Turkish Airlines domestic flights in Turkey, or EasyJet), put the fare and taxes together in one line. UDMS won't have that currency, so convert it to US dollars or rand, add a little extra, enter the buy price and leave the sell price blank.

If an airline is on Amadeus but not loaded in UDMS (as with some South American flights), it won't pull through, so load it on Flight Carrier the same way.

Flight Allocations

We only use flight allocations in our peak seasons, mainly SAA and Air Mauritius for Mauritius. Sometimes there are set departures for Zanzibar too, depending on the season.

Air Mauritius
Pulls through automaticallyBook it in the normal flight search. Negotiated means it's our rate. Published is the normal public fare, often around double.
SAA
Booked on Flight CarrierSearch with only the departure, arrival and dates (no airline). The loaded allocation shows its price and how many seats are left.
  1. Check availability: ticketing sends an email listing allocation dates that still have seats.
  2. Pull the allocation through on Flight Carrier and tick both the fare and the taxes.
  3. Get the real airport taxes from Amadeus. The taxes on the allocation aren't kept up to date, so price the same SAA flights in Amadeus and use those taxes.
  4. Add the taxes per person on a separate taxes line, as in Step 2. Adults and children pay different taxes, so check both.
  5. To book: change the quote to a booking and request it. Ticketing books it on Amadeus in their group booking and sends it to you. Once the client has paid, ask ticketing to issue.
🎫
Tickets are issued straight away nowWith SAA and Air Mauritius allocations, clients no longer wait until a few days before travel. Once paid and issued, the tickets come through immediately.
⚠️
Always include the taxesIt's easy to quote an allocation without its airport taxes. Check the taxes line is there before you send the quote.
❓
To confirmWho sends the allocation availability email (the name isn't clear in the recording).

Hotels: Tariffs and APIs

4

Hotel Tariffs and Allocations

A tariff is our loaded contract rate with the hotel. Some hotels also give us inventory (an allocation of rooms). When we do buy allocation, it's usually only 3 to 5 rooms. A figure of 99 doesn't mean 99 rooms; it means the hotel hasn't told us of any stop sell yet.

Green: good to goWe still have rooms and haven't heard of any stop sells. Book with confidence.
Amber: partialSome nights are blocked out or limited. Click it and scroll to your dates to see which nights.
Red: no more rooms in our inventoryThis doesn't mean the hotel is full. It means it's on request: check the hotel's website or phone them.
  • You can quote a red or amber hotel on our contract rate, but check availability before you book.
  • If a client asks for one specific hotel, check availability before you even quote.
  • Rate queries: hover over the rate to see it in the hotel's currency. Check it matches our contract before taking it up with the supplier.
🎄
Take extra care over Christmas, New Year and EasterIn very high season, a hotel's contract may say our rate falls away once they send a stop sell, even if they still have rooms. Phone the hotel to confirm they will honour our rate. This happens most with Mozambique, Thailand and Bali, and with European and Far East hotels.
💡
Dynamic ratesSome hotels (for example in Zanzibar) have dynamic rates. If the API shows a higher rate than the tariff, use the API rate.

5

Hotel APIs

API results come live from the hotel's own system, so the availability is real and the rates are guaranteed. You can see exactly how many rooms are left.

  • Need a quick confirmation? Use the API.
  • The tariff and API rate should be close. Small differences of a few hundred rand are usually the rate of exchange.
  • Check the rate type. Some rates in UDMS are master rates for non-South African residents. Don't use a non-resident rate for South African clients; the API is usually cheaper anyway.
🔁
Remember the peak season ruleNo inventory doesn't mean fully booked, and you can still use the rate. Over Christmas, New Year and Easter, confirm with the hotel first.

Packages, Tours and Suppliers

6

Packages

Set packages (for example Egypt, Italy by rail and Vietnam) are a guideline. The hotels aren't always guaranteed, especially at busy times or big events, when the supplier may need a different hotel and add a supplement. The only packages we haven't had issues with are Turkey.

  • Check availability on the rate before you tell the client to pay.
  • Packages often don't name the hotel (for example "4-star in Cairo"). This is on purpose.
  • Check what's included: transfers, meals, domestic flights and tours.
🖥️
Web enquiry with a TH number?Don't just copy the package across and recalculate. Open the package on one screen and UDMS on the other, and build it manually item by item. Check every hotel is still available, and always recheck the flights and transfers.

Tours and Choosing Suppliers

Tours is where you find sightseeing tours for a city, and guided tours on set dates. Packages aren't fully guided and don't run on set dates; some include a guide for sightseeing only.

  • Guided tours have a minimum number of people. Read the tour rules (for example minimum 6, maximum 34). If the tour doesn't fill up, it may not run, even after it's confirmed. Make sure the client knows.
  • Quoting for two people? Change the room to a double, or the tour may quote for more people than you need.
  • Ask the supplier which hotel they will confirm, or ask for the specific hotel you quoted. "Same star rating" doesn't always mean the same quality.
  • Check whether flights are included, and add any flights needed to get clients to the start of the package.
🤝
Book with our own supplier where we have oneIf we have a supplier in that country, book as much as possible with them, so they can sort out any issues and everything is on one voucher. Hotels that pull through on Destinations of the World vary in quality, so check the hotel and its location before you use one.

Confirmations and Last-Minute Bookings

8

API References and Last-Minute Bookings

  1. Request the API booking. A green tick and a reference appear next to it.
  2. Click the item to open its details and scroll down to the channel manager reference.
  3. Sun City (and some Cape Town hotels): this reference is reliable, so you can use it to confirm straight away.
  4. International APIs (for example Tour East): the channel manager reference isn't always right, so wait for the supplier to add theirs.
⏱️
Last-minute bookings Accommodation must be confirmed before you issue flights.
Clients pay by card, because a bank transfer can take a day or two to reflect.
Take full ownership: don't leave a booking that travels tomorrow for someone else to finish.
For international bookings, allow at least two days, because of time differences and weekends.
💬
Quick quotes while on chatChat always comes first, but if something quick like Sun City comes in, send it out while you're on chat rather than leaving it until later. Speed comes with practice; quality still matters.

✓

Final Check Before Sending

  • FastJet booked on the FastJet website, not through Hahn Air
  • Website flights loaded on Flight Carrier with the fare per person and the sell price left blank
  • Airport taxes on their own line, with the same buy and sell price
  • Flights in another currency loaded as one line, converted with a little extra
  • SAA allocation taxes checked in Amadeus, and a taxes line added
  • Red or amber hotels: availability checked before booking
  • Christmas, New Year or Easter: hotel confirmed it will honour our rate
  • Package hotels, flights and transfers rechecked, not just copied
  • Tour minimum numbers explained to the client
  • Last minute: accommodation confirmed before flights are issued, and paid by card
